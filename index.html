Model notes

What this prototype does

Converts the clinician-selected pre-test probability to odds, multiplies by each selected likelihood ratio, and converts back to probability. It is intended for education and model development.

What it does not do

It is not validated for patient-level diagnosis, antibiotic decisions, neonates, immunocompromised children, ICU-level disease, chronic lung disease, or local epidemiology adjustments.

Why respiratory testing is special

Upper-airway PCR can identify pathogens, colonization, shedding, or codetection. Therefore respiratory panel results are interpretation-only by default; the heuristic toggle is for sensitivity analysis.

Not medical advice. Use clinical judgment and local guidelines.
