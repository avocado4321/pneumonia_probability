Scope

Model notes

What this prototype does

Converts the clinician-selected pre-test probability to odds, multiplies by each selected likelihood ratio, and converts back to probability.

What it does not do

It is not validated for patient-level diagnosis, antibiotic decisions, neonates, immunocompromised children, ICU-level disease, chronic lung disease, or local epidemiology adjustments.

Why respiratory testing is special

Upper-airway PCR can identify pathogens, colonization, shedding, or codetection. Respiratory panel results are interpretation-only by default.

Not medical advice. Use clinical judgment and local guidelines.
